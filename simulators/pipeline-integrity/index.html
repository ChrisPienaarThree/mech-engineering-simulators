    Remaining strength of corroded pipe (862.213, Appendix L)

    A = 0.893 L / √(D t)  ·  A ≤ 4: P′ = 1.1 P (1 − ⅔ d/t) / (1 − ⅔ (d/t) / √(A² + 1))  ·  A > 4: P′ = 1.1 P (1 − d/t)  ·  P′ ≤ P

    For lines at 40 % SMYS or more. P is the greater of the design pressure (without E) and the established MAOP. Depth under 10 % of wall: no reduction. Over 80 %: consider taking the segment out of service. If the MAOP is above P′, reduce the MAOP or repair (851.4) or replace.

    Why the curve jumps at A = 4

    Original B31G treats a short feature as a parabola (⅔ of the depth times the length) and a long one as a rectangle of full depth. The two formulas do not meet at A = 4, so the acceptance curve steps down there. This is a known feature of the method and one reason the modified method was developed.

    Modified B31G (comparison only)

    z = L²/(D t) · M = √(1 + 0.6275 z − 0.003375 z²) (z ≤ 50), else 0.032 z + 3.3 · Sf = (SMYS + 69 MPa)(1 − 0.85 d/t)/(1 − 0.85 (d/t)/M) · P′ = F · 2 Sf t / D

    Not in B31.8-2003. Shown so you can see why the later method is less conservative. [Check against ASME B31G in force before use.]

    Cathodic protection criteria (862.214, Appendix K = NACE RP0169 §6)

    −0.85 V to Cu/CuSO₄ with current applied (IR drop considered) · or 300 mV shift with current applied · or 100 mV polarisation from the instant-off reading

    A potential measured with the current on includes the IR drop through the soil. In high-resistivity ground the on reading can look protected while the pipe itself is not polarised (6.2.4). Interrupt the rectifier and read the instant-off potential. Very negative off potentials (more negative than about −1.2 V) risk coating disbondment and hydrogen damage [industry practice, not in B31.8].

    How far the current reaches

    α = √(r G) · r = ρsteel / (π (D − t) t) · G = g · π D

    The pipe is a leaky conductor: current flows along the steel and leaks off through the coating. Good coating (low conductance g) lets one rectifier protect tens of kilometres; poor coating soaks up current close to the drain point. The model solves this as a resistor ladder every 100 m with insulating flanges at both ends.

    Repairs (851.44)

    (a) Take out of service and cut out a cylinder of pipe. (b) Full encirclement welded split sleeve. (c) Bolt-on leak clamp for a corrosion-pit leak. (e) Steel plate patch only on pipe of 40 000 psi (276 MPa) SMYS or less, no more than half the circumference. (f) Weld deposit on small corroded areas only where the remaining wall is above the burn-through table and the pipe is not brittle. Every repair is tested and inspected (851.5).

    Interference (862.215)

    Current picked up from a DC railway leaves the pipe where it crosses back toward the rail substation, and corrodes the pipe at that point. Find it from the potentials and mitigate it, here with a drainage bond.

    Remaining life and re-inspection

    Each feature grows at the corrosion rate where it sits. Its life ends when P′ falls to the MAOP or the depth reaches 80 %. The model sets the next inspection at half the shortest remaining life, at most 10 years. [Illustrative rule: operators set intervals by their integrity plan, ASME B31.8S.]

    About the numbers

    [Illustrative data, for training only.] Clauses follow ASME B31.8-2003. Soil, coating conductances, corrosion rates, IR fractions, the railway and every feature are invented. Free corrosion rate ≈ 0.6/(1 + ρ/15) mm/y at a holiday; polarisation reduces it by e per 35 mV.

    How to use the assessor

    • CP survey: the plot shows the on and instant-off potentials along 20 km. Click it to read a test post. Raise the rectifier current until the whole route meets a criterion, without going below −1.2 V off.
    • Features: the bars under the plot are corrosion features from an in-line inspection, height = depth. Click one, or use the Anomaly list. The readout shows A, the safe pressure P′ and the verdict.
    • Decide: accept and monitor, sleeve, cut out, patch or weld deposit, or reduce the MAOP. The list says whether your choice is valid.
    • Time: +5 years grows every unrepaired feature at its local corrosion rate. Weak CP, poor coating or the railway will turn features into failures.

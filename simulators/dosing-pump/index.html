How to use this simulator

  • Choose a system: each has its own chemical, stream flow, injection pressure and pump size.
  • Work out the dose: the dose calculator gives the pump rate from dose, stream flow and product strength.
  • Set and start: set stroke and speed, then start the pump.
  • Prove it by drawdown: fill the column, close the tank valve, time at least 60 s and read the column at start and stop. Rate (L/h) = mL ÷ s × 3.6. Restore before the column empties.
  • Find faults: load a scenario code or a Mystery fault. Confirm the symptom, walk the skid in flow order, change one thing, then submit your diagnosis.
  • Safety: on a real pump, isolate, depressurise and decontaminate before opening it, and follow the OEM manual and site procedure.

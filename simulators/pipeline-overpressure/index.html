    Protection is required (845.1, 845.212, 845.222)

    Any pipeline or system fed from a source that could exceed its MAOP if pressure control failed needs pressure-relieving or pressure-limiting devices. For pipelines: spring-loaded relief valves, pilot-loaded back-pressure regulators that open if the pilot fails, rupture discs. For high-pressure distribution also: a monitoring regulator in series with the primary regulator, a series regulator, or an automatic shut-off set at or below MAOP that stays shut until reset by hand.

    How far above MAOP (845.411)

    hoop over 72 % SMYS: MAOP + 4 %  ·  at or below 72 %: lesser of MAOP + 10 % and the pressure at 75 % SMYS

    The protective devices must have the capacity and settings to keep the system under this limit. Where a second regulator works in series with the first, checks must show that the pressure stays at or below MAOP if any one regulator fails wide open (845.421).

    Station design (841.126)

    Overpressure protection by a monitor regulator in series with each controlling regulator, adequately sized relief valves downstream, or overpressure shut-off valves. Separate isolation for each regulator's supply, control and sensing lines. Prevent freezing of regulators and pilots. Avoid sound levels of 110 dBA and above. Keep gas velocity below 100 ft/s (30 m/s) at peak.

    Isolation and single incidents (845.34–845.36)

    Lock the stop valve under a relief valve open, or fit duplicate relief valves so only one can be isolated at a time. Design so that a single incident cannot disable both the regulator and its protection: control and sensing lines must be protected, and damage to one line must not disable both.

    The model

    Q = K · y · P1 · √(1 − (P2/P1)²), choked below P2/P1 = 0.53  ·  dP2/dt = (Qin − Qdemand − Qrelief) · pstd / Vpack

    Monitor and worker are in series; the pressure between them is solved so both pass the same flow. Each regulator integrates the error between its set point and the pressure on its sensing line. Relief lifts from its set point to full capacity at 10 % accumulation. Joule–Thomson cooling ≈ 0.5 °C per bar of let-down.

    How to use the station

    • Normal day: the worker regulator holds the outlet at its set point while customer demand rises and falls. The monitor sits wide open, waiting.
    • Set points: worker below monitor, monitor at or below MAOP, relief and slam-shut above them but within the allowed overpressure. The checks under the readouts mark each rule.
    • Inject a failure and watch which device catches it. Repair and reset the slam-shut by hand. Reset the highest reading before each new test.
    • Design: remove the monitor and slam-shut and size a relief valve alone; try the old spur at 80 % SMYS; leave the relief isolated; share the sensing line; let the gas go cold.
